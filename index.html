🐱 Feline & Canine 🐶

Surprising facts about our best friends
🐈

Cat Facts

🐾 Cats can make over 100 different vocal sounds. Dogs only make about 10.
👃 A cat's nose print is unique, just like a human fingerprint. No two cats have the same pattern.
💤 Cats spend about 70% of their lives sleeping. That's roughly 13–16 hours a day to recharge.
🧠 Cats have a specialized collarbone that allows them to always land on their feet — the righting reflex.
👑 Ancient Egyptians revered cats as sacred. Killing one was punishable by death.
🐕

Dog Facts

👃 A dog's sense of smell is 10,000–100,000 times stronger than a human's. They can detect certain diseases.
🎓 Dogs can learn over 250 words and gestures. The smartest breeds can understand up to 1,000 words.
🌡️ Dogs sweat through their paw pads — that's why they pant heavily to cool down.
❤️ Dogs have a "puppy dog eyes" muscle (the levator anguli oculi medialis) that evolved specifically to bond with humans.
🐕‍🦺 Basenjis and New Guinea singing dogs are the only breeds that don't bark — they yodel, whine, or howl.
